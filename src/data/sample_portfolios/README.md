# Sample portfolios

Test data for Finnie. The tickers are real, but the share counts and average costs are **made up** for illustration. They do not describe a real account.

Columns: `ticker, shares, avg_cost` (average price paid per share, in dollars).

- `diversified.csv`: broad U.S. stocks, international stocks, bonds, real estate, one individual stock.
- `concentrated_single_stock.csv`: most of the money in one stock.
- `bond_heavy.csv`: mostly bond funds with a small stock holding.
