# The Saver's Credit

The Saver's Credit (formally the Retirement Savings Contributions Credit) is a tax credit for people who contribute to an IRA or an employer-sponsored retirement plan. This article summarizes the IRS page checked on 2026-10-31. The income table on that page was for tax year 2024 and income limits change each year, so this article does not repeat the table; check the current IRS figures.

## Who may claim it
You must meet all three conditions:
- you are 18 or older;
- someone else does not claim you as a dependent on their return;
- you are not a student (generally, enrolled full-time during any part of 5 calendar months of the tax year).

## How the credit is figured
The credit equals 50%, 20% or 10% of your eligible contributions. Your adjusted gross income (AGI) decides which percentage applies, and above a cutoff the credit is 0%.

## What counts as an eligible contribution
- Traditional or Roth IRA contributions
- Elective deferrals to a 401(k), 403(b), governmental 457(b), SARSEP or SIMPLE plan
- Voluntary after-tax contributions to a qualified retirement plan or 403(b) plan
- Contributions to an ABLE account, for the designated beneficiary

Rollover contributions do not qualify, and recent distributions from a retirement plan, IRA or ABLE account can reduce your eligible contributions.
