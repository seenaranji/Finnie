# Series EE Savings Bonds

Series EE savings bonds are U.S. Treasury savings bonds that the government calls "a low-risk way to save money." This article is based on the TreasuryDirect page checked on 2026-10-31. It does not list the current rate, because rates change; see TreasuryDirect.

## How they earn interest
Interest accrues monthly and compounds every six months. For bonds issued since May 2005, "the bond's interest rate is set when you buy the bond," and it stays fixed for the first 20 years. TreasuryDirect also says it guarantees that bonds bought under its current terms will double in value in 20 years.

## Key rules
- **Buying:** newer EE bonds are electronic only, through a TreasuryDirect account. The minimum is $25, and each Social Security Number may buy up to $10,000 in EE bonds per calendar year.
- **Holding period:** interest is earned for up to 30 years.
- **Cashing early:** you can cash a bond after 12 months, but cashing before 5 years means giving up the last 3 months of interest.

## Taxes
Earnings are subject to federal income tax but not state or local income tax. You can choose to report interest each year or when you cash the bond. Earnings may be exempt from federal tax if used for qualified higher education expenses.

This article is general education, not advice about what you personally should do.
