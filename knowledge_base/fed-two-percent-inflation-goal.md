# Why the Federal Reserve Aims for 2 Percent Inflation

This article is based on a Federal Reserve FAQ page checked on 2026-10-31.

## The goal
The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) judges that inflation of 2 percent over the longer run, measured by the annual change in the personal consumption expenditures (PCE) price index, is "most consistent with the Federal Reserve's mandate for maximum employment and price stability."

## The reasons given on the page
- **A predictable backdrop:** when households and businesses "can reasonably expect inflation to remain low and stable," they can make sound decisions about saving, borrowing and investment.
- **A broader benefit:** according to the page, those sound decisions help the economy work well and support the well-being of Americans.

## What the page does not say
It does not discuss deflation or very low inflation. It states only that the goal is inflation that stays low and stable.

This article is general education, not advice about what you personally should do.
