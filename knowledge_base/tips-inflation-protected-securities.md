# TIPS: Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities

TIPS are U.S. Treasury securities designed to protect investors against inflation. This article is based on the TreasuryDirect page checked on 2026-10-31.

## How TIPS work
- **Terms:** 5, 10 or 30 years.
- **Principal changes:** unlike most Treasury securities, the principal (face value) changes. It rises with inflation and falls with deflation, using a version of the Consumer Price Index from the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
- **Interest:** a fixed rate set at auction, paid every six months. Because the rate is applied to the adjusted principal, each payment can be a different amount. TreasuryDirect says the rate is never less than 0.125%.
- **At maturity:** you receive the inflation-adjusted principal or the original principal, whichever is greater, so you never get back less than the original amount.
- **Buying:** the minimum is $100, in $100 steps; TIPS are issued in electronic form only and are eligible for STRIPS.

## Taxes
Federal tax is due each year on the interest earned, and changes in the principal during the year may also affect federal taxes. There are no state or local taxes.

## Things to know
- If inflation turns to deflation, the principal can fall during the term, but the maturity guarantee limits the loss to the original amount.
- Interest payments vary because they are based on the adjusted principal.
- The page does not explain how market prices move if you sell before maturity.

This article is general education, not advice about what you personally should do.
