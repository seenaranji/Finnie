# The Goals of U.S. Monetary Policy

This article is based on the Federal Reserve Board's monetary policy page, checked on 2026-10-31.

## The goals
Congress has given the Federal Reserve specific economic goals. U.S. monetary policy aims to achieve "maximum employment, stable prices, and moderate long-term interest rates."

## Who carries it out
The Federal Reserve, which the Board describes as "the central bank of the United States," conducts monetary policy through its actions and its public communications. The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) issues policy statements, minutes and holds press conferences.

## The tools
The page lists several tools, including open market operations, the discount window, reserve requirements and interest on reserve balances. Each has its own article in this knowledge base where available.

## Reviewing the framework
In 2025 the Fed began a periodic review of the framework it uses to pursue its "congressionally-assigned goals of maximum employment and price stability."

## Why investors pay attention
Monetary policy affects interest rates, which can affect bond prices, borrowing costs and the broader economy.
